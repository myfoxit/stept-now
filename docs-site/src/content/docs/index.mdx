## How it fits together

A **Workspace** is your tenant. **Inboxes** are channel instances (widget, email, Slack,
Telegram, API) that feed **Conversations** with your **Contacts**. **Knowledge sources**
ingest content into documents and chunks for retrieval. The **AI agent** sits on an inbox,
answers from knowledge, runs **tools**, and escalates to your team when needed. **Tours**
guide users through your product, and the **MCP server** exposes the whole workspace to AI
clients like Claude.
